## Frequently asked questions

### How does a scan work?

1. Record your cat in CatVox (up to 10 seconds), or pick a clip from your Photos library (up to 10 seconds and 100 MB).
2. CatVox uploads the clip, with its sound, to Google Cloud. There, Google's Gemini AI model looks at your cat's body language and listens to its sounds.
3. A few moments later you get your cat's main emotion, a confidence score, a short analysis, a persona, your cat's "thought" and a tip. The result is saved in your scan history on your iPhone.

You need an internet connection to scan.

### How many free scans do I get?

Five a day. The count starts again at midnight UTC (Coordinated Universal Time). Scans that fail don't count towards the five. The home screen shows how many you have left today.

### Is a CatVox result veterinary advice?

No. CatVox's results are generated by AI as behavioural insight and entertainment. They can be wrong, and they aren't veterinary advice, a diagnosis or a treatment. If you're worried about your cat's health or behaviour, for example if it seems unwell or in pain, please talk to a vet.

### Why was my video rejected?

CatVox can analyse clips that are:

- 10 seconds long or shorter;
- 100 MB or smaller;
- recorded as H.264 or HEVC video, the formats iPhones normally use. ProRes video isn't supported.

You can trim a longer video in the Photos app and then pick it again.

### Does CatVox keep my video?

The clip you analyse is uploaded to Google Cloud in the United States and deleted automatically once it's a day old. **[PLACEHOLDER Q4: keep this in step with the Privacy Policy's wording on clip deletion]** We use it only to produce your result, and Google's terms don't allow Google to use it to train AI models without our permission, which we don't give. Your result is stored only on your iPhone. The [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) has the details.

### How do I delete a scan?

Tap the trash icon next to the scan in your history, then tap **Delete**. This removes CatVox's copy of the clip, its result and any share video made from it. The original video stays in your Photos library.

### How do I delete all my data?

Deleting the CatVox app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Clips on our servers are deleted automatically. To have your usage record and analytics data deleted too, email us your install ID, shown in **[PLACEHOLDER Q7: e.g. CatVox → About (P25)]**, before you delete the app. See [Deleting your data](privacy.md#deleting-your-data) in the Privacy Policy.

### Why does CatVox ask for my camera and microphone?

To record clips of your cat. The sound helps CatVox pick up meows, purrs and hisses. CatVox can still record without the microphone, but the analysis then has no sound to work with. CatVox only asks to add videos to your Photos library when you save a share video.

### CatVox says it couldn't verify the app

Before it uploads a clip, CatVox checks that it's a genuine copy running on a real iPhone. Make sure you installed CatVox from the App Store, check your internet connection and try again later. If it keeps happening, contact us.

### A scan failed

Check your internet connection, then tap **Retry**. Failed scans don't use up your daily scans.
